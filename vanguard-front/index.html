01 / Game overview

A homeworld is only the beginning.

Vanguard Front combines the long-term decisions of empire-building strategy with real-time fleet logistics and diplomacy. Commanders develop colonies, manage production and construction, specialize through research, and decide how to allocate resources between infrastructure, defense, exploration and naval power.

Progress is connected: industry supplies the drydock, research opens new capabilities, logistics links the colonies, and fleet operations put the results of those decisions to the test. The game runs in a web browser, with an online account and an evolving shared universe.

Your charter, your strategy

  • Choose research and shipyard priorities.

02 / World and history

The lanes have begun to hum again.

The Meridian Reach is a ring of 499 star systems surrounding a dead, silent core. Once connected by the Lane Dominion, the colonies were severed when the Resonant Shiver fractured the central gate at Meridian Zero.

THE LANE DOMINION

The age of connection

Eleven thousand gates once linked distant worlds. Commerce, travel and knowledge moved freely until the collapse broke the network.

THE HOLLOW SILENCE

Centuries of isolation

Cut off from each other, worlds survived through scavenging and fragmented archives. The origins of the catastrophe remain unknown.

THE DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

A new frontier

The Meridian Compact grants charters to commanders capable of establishing colonies and holding lanes. The traces of older builders—and the Aether found in relics—offer mysteries beyond the known worlds.

03 / Gameplay systems

Every decision has a logistical consequence.

01 · ECONOMY

Planetary extraction

Metal, crystal and deuterium power your empire. Deep-Seam Extractors, Lattice Harvesters and Isotope Refineries produce resources over time. Balance the energy grid and expand storage before capacity limits interrupt production.

02 · INFRASTRUCTURE

Develop the colony

Automaton Foundries accelerate construction. Swarm Nanoforges support advanced fabrication. Orbital Drydocks unlock vessels and defenses, and Axiom Laboratories advance your research.

03 · SCIENCE

Research and specialization

Develop Burn, Pulse and Fold Drives for different ship classes. Armament Engineering, Barrier Theory and Hull Metallurgy improve battle capabilities, while Stellar Cartography supports expansion and exploration.

04 · FLEET

Build a balanced armada

Courier Haulers and Bulk Freighters carry materials; Dart Interceptors, Warden Cruisers and Ironclad Battleships fill different combat roles. Whisper Probes scout targets, Seedship Arks establish colonies, and Salvage Reclaimers recover wreckage.

05 · MISSIONS

Operations across the Reach

Dispatch fleets for transport, deployment, espionage, attack, colonization, salvage and deep-space exploration. Travel times, cargo capacity, fuel and target information matter when coordinating missions.

06 · DEFENSE

Protect your holdings

Build planetary batteries, turrets and ward domes. Monitor the radar for incoming missions and assess the risks of leaving resources or ships exposed. Combat and reconstruction rules are detailed in the field manual.

07 · COMBAT

Fleet encounters

Documented battle mechanics include tactical rounds, rapid-fire interactions, plunder, orbital wreckfields and possible moon formation. The Quantum Battle Simulator offers a separate way to examine prospective fleet matchups before committing assets.

08 · DIPLOMACY

Alliance Combat System

Form or join alliances, manage ranks and recruitment, coordinate joint strikes, and station allied fleets for defense. Commander profiles, communication and alliance portals connect the strategic and social layers.

09 · LOGISTICS

Advanced empire management

The documented advanced systems include Imperial Freight, planetary relocation, High Command officers and lunar facilities. Aether services have distinct costs and conditions. Check current in-game rules before using them.

Need exact mechanics? The Commander’s Tactical Field Manual contains the detailed source-documented formulas, mission directives, cancellation rules, officer systems, events and coalition operations. Balance values and display names can change.

04 / Sovereign doctrines

Choose the kind of commander you want to become.

INDUSTRY

Consortium Collector

The Provisioners' Consortium represents miners, haulers and brokers. Its fantasy is prosperity through resource production, industrial infrastructure and trade.

WARFARE

Vanguard General

The Marshals of the Reach are fleet captains and lane wardens. Their doctrine emphasizes disciplined naval strength, tactical positioning and defense.

EXPLORATION

Deep Space Discoverer

The Pathseekers are survey crews and relic hunters. Their pursuit is discovery beyond the familiar lanes, including expeditions and traces of the older builders.

Doctrine names and descriptions follow the project's setting and guide. Consult the live game for current bonuses and selection rules.

05 / Community and support

The Front is built with its commanders.

Independent development

Vanguard Front is a WildCat Studio project supported by voluntary community contributions. Support helps fund continuing gameplay improvements, infrastructure and development. The game's support initiative is designed to avoid predatory gacha and keep donations separate from competitive stat boosts.

In-game Aether, officers and other services have their own rules; this is not a claim that every in-game benefit is free or purely cosmetic.

Admiralty Archives

Supporters can include their commander callsign with a contribution to request recognition in the Admiralty Archives. The official support terminal explains current patron honors and contribution options. Participation is optional.

06 / Development

A connected universe, built to evolve.

Technical foundation

The game uses a Laravel-based web application and browser UI. Resource progression, scheduled fleet and construction events, and responsive command displays are designed to keep ongoing empire activity understandable. The project emphasizes performance, data integrity and ongoing refinement.

Roadmap direction

The development material describes continued expansion of Jumpgate Logistics Network capabilities, celestial anomalies, tactical radar and alliance operations. These are development directions, not guaranteed delivery dates or announcements that each feature is live.

07 / FAQ

Commander questions.

Do I need to install a game client?

Vanguard Front is a browser-based online game. Visit the official site to register or sign in and access the command deck.

What can I do besides combat?

Build industry, manage resources, research technology, establish colonies, run cargo logistics, explore and form alliances.

Is the showcase a playable version of the game?

No. This is the WildCat Studio project showcase and documentation mirror. Play on the official Vanguard Front website.

Are the guide's prices and statistics always current?

No. The guide is a snapshot of the documented game. Universe settings, balance, content names and published features may differ. Use the current in-game field manual when planning a mission or spending resources.

How can I support the project?

Use the official support terminal, report bugs through the game's supported channels and participate in the community. Donations are optional.
